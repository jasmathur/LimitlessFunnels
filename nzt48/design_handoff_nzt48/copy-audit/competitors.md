Checked 2026-10-18. This is for creative benchmarking only. Claim phrasings are summarized and are not to be reused as copy.

Confidence levels:
- **High:** read directly from the brand's own Supplement Facts image or text.
- **Med:** from the brand's directions or marketing copy, or from multiple retailer panels.
- **UNVERIFIED:** not confirmed.

## 2. Positioning and claims

| Brand | Positioning | Benefit hierarchy (as ordered on PDP) | Claim style |
|---|---|---|---|
| Alpha BRAIN | Caffeine-free daily cognitive support, framed around "flow" and alpha waves, with a Rogan co-founder halo | Focus/memory > processing speed > daily support > relaxed alertness/flow | "Promotes/Supports/Aids X†" with the DSHEA disclaimer. Black Label is the premium tier. |
| Qualia Mind | Premium "complete" nootropic stack (many ingredients) with a cycling protocol (5 days on, 2 off) | Focus & concentration > mood & motivation > productivity/alertness > memory & long-term brain health | "Boosts/Supports/Promotes X*" plus a benefit timeline from day 1–2 to "years" |
| Mind Lab Pro | "Universal nootropic" for every audience segment | Processing speed and recall stats (study %) > "11 nootropics / 6 pathways" | Study-stat-led claims. Note that two of the three studies were brand-funded. |
| NooCube | Value all-in-one daily nootropic "for people with deadlines" | Focus > problem-solving > memory/alertness > screen-fatigue eye support > brain fog | Aggressive phrasing ("razor sharp", "zero known side effects", "FDA-approved ingredients"). This is risky and is a pattern to avoid. |
| Neuriva | Mass-retail "brain health/performance" with ingredient trademarks (Cognicell, Neurofactor) | Memory (3 types) > focus/accuracy > learning | "Shown to support X" plus "clinically tested ingredient" |
