## A. Blockers for the coordinator (assets and data you own)

1. **The 3D bottle label is an old, different formula.** `3d/label-v2/source-label.png` (wrapped on the GLB the user sees in the hero, serving, final CTA and other sections) has:
   - "60 CAPS" on the front but "Servings Per Container 30" on the panel. It contradicts itself, and the hero shows it next to "30 capsules".
   - A blend list that does not match the page: it adds **Synephrine HCL and Vinpocetine** and leaves out Guarana, Phosphatidylserine and Uridine-5 Monophosphate.
   - Front claims "Optimize mental agility · Increase cognitive power · Sharpen focus & memory · Elevate mood & positivity". The back says "boost creativity, solve problems, stay positive…", "Physician formulated", "Manufactured in an FDA registered facility", "Stock Ticker: VYBE", "EXP 12/2023" and www.thelimitlesspill.com.
   - The front claims can be read at 320–430px in the hero (I checked with a zoomed screenshot).
2. **The fallback `assets/bottle.png` has a third label:** "The Ultimate Brain Booster · Improve memory · Amplify focus · Elevate mood", 30 caps. It appears when WebGL fails, and it is used as the cart thumbnail (`{{ still }}`). Memory and mood claims go beyond the claim matrix.
   → The label art needs to match the approved shipping label (this ties to LAUNCH-NOTES open item 2). I didn't check the lifestyle and packshot photos (`assets/photos/*.webp`) for label text. They show the bottle (for example `l-twohands` in Who It Fits) and need the same check.
3. **Size range:** the command says this is a 30-count funnel and not to add 60/90 "unless already approved". V1 sells 30/60/90: the hero selector, the Bundles section, the cart upsell ("Switch to 60 capsules…"), and the FAQ "difference between 30, 60 and 90". It needs a decision.
4. **Competitor counts:** `D.compare` and the FAQ say Qualia Mind® is **6** capsules. The command's proposed contrast says Qualia is **4**. They have to be re-verified before either number is used.
5. **Unverified trust claims** used on V1 (LAUNCH-NOTES item 7): "Made in the USA · GMP-certified facility" in the marquee (l.50) and the hero trust row (l.149).
6. **Guarantee window** of 30 days (item 5, conflicts with the 90-day badge) appears 6 times: the marquee, hero trust row, Subscribe vs One-Time, Guarantee, Final CTA and the FAQ.
7. **The Returns Policy link is broken:** `../../pages/returns-exchanges/index.html` returns 404 (Guarantee l.471, footer l.512). It needs the live URL.

## D. Presentation baseline

Tested in the in-app Chromium with resize_window at 320×700, 360×740, 375×812, 390×844, 414×896, 430×932, 768×1024 and 1440×900, in Dark and Light (`data-theme`). Each width had a full scroll pass, then a DOM probe for horizontal overflow, targets under 44px and clipped text, plus a computed-contrast pass in both themes. **Not tested:** real iOS Safari or Android, landscape, notch safe areas on a device, keyboard overlap, a forced reduced-motion run, or forced WebGL failure. Reduced motion and the fallback were reviewed in code only.

### Overflow / clipping
- **Header "Shop NZT48" is cut off at 320px and 360px.** `header > div > nav > a[href="#buy"]` ends at x=364 (44px past the edge at 320, 4px at 360) and is clipped by the root `overflow-x:clip`. The logo is 22px tall with 24px padding, and `nav` has no shrink. The page doesn't scroll sideways (docW = viewport at every width).
- At 320px the hero eyebrow "Nootropic capsules · 750mg blend" wraps to 2 lines, and "Choose your supply / 1 capsule a day" wraps onto 2 lines each (`#buy > div:first-child`).
- Subscribe vs One-Time `h2` "Buy once, no commitment" overflows its box by 2px at 320 (222 vs 220).
- The 768px tablet gets the phone layout (`wide = w ≥ 820`): the bottle stacks above the buy box, and Who It Fits rows are 728px wide. That works, but tablet uses the space poorly.

### Tap targets (<44px)
- Hero `select[aria-label="Delivery frequency"]` is 163×40 at every width.
- `#demanding-days .dd-row` is 33px tall on phones and tablet (44 on desktop).
- Guarantee `a[href*="returns"]` (inline, 23px) and footer Returns link (18px). Both are inline text links, so this is a minor issue.
- Desktop header nav links `header nav a[href="#formula"|"#facts"|"#faq"]` are 14px tall.
- The theme-switch FAB (`version-switch.js`, shared and preview-only) is 40×40.

### Contrast (WCAG AA)
- `#demanding-days .dd-row` inactive rows use `rgba(var(--t243_241_236),.34)`: **2.79:1 in dark, 2.10:1 in light** (24px uppercase, needs 3:1). This is intentional dimming, but it fails.
- Light theme: hero subscribe strike price (`#buy` subscribe row, 12.5px, `--c85888F`) is 3.33:1.
- Everything else measured passed in both themes. Text over the photo stage and the gradient cards was skipped by the tool; visually, light theme looks readable on Who It Fits, the blue Subscribe card and Comparison.

### Sticky CTA
- It appears below 900px after scrollY > 700 and hides while the cart is open ✔. It respects `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)` ✔.
- Its label "Add to cart" doesn't match the hero CTA `{{ cta }}`.
- **The shared theme FAB overlaps content** on phones: it sits over the right edge of the hero sub-line, the "1 capsule a day" label, and the Comparison "6" count, just above the sticky bar. It's preview-only, but it hides content during review.

### Reduced motion (code review)
- CSS `@media (prefers-reduced-motion)` kills all animations and transitions ✔. Both marquees stop, so the top bar shows a static, cut-off strip ("…DAY MONEY-BACK GUARANTEE | MADE…"). The rotating messages become unreadable, and the decorative "The Limitless Pill" band is static.
- The stage respects `reduced` (no spin, reveal or bottling sequence) ✔. Who It Fits auto-advance stops ✔.

### 3D fallback (code review)
- `[data-nzt-fallback]` images are shown only when `initThree()` rejects (`3d/nzt-stage.js:581–584`). **If the module import itself fails** (HTML l.599 `.catch` only logs), all fallback images stay at `opacity:0`, so no bottle appears anywhere.
- The desktop sticky anchors `[data-nzt-anchor="benefits"|"formula"|"facts"]` have **no fallback image**, so without WebGL those columns are empty.
- The fallback image is the old-label PNG (A2).

### Other
- The visible hero lead is "NZT48 Original" (Boldonse 26–36px). The campaign line isn't readable above the fold on any width (3-second test: what it is ✔ via the eyebrow and sub; "The Limitless Pill" ✘).
- The cart `img src="{{ still }}"` placeholder shows briefly before the stage sets `__nztStill` (harmless, noted earlier).

Every marketing string now comes from `D.copy`, `D.*` or computed values: marquee, hero, trust row, One Capsule (including the stats), Benefits, Ingredients labels, Routine, Who It Fits (copy.uses.eyebrow added), Comparison (foot dated Oct 18), Supplement Facts panel, Reviews (eyebrow, line, reviewsUrl), Bundles (lede added), Plans (rates and onceBody computed in code), Guarantee ("30-day returns" badge and body), FAQ (lede and health line added), Final CTA, footer (blurb and legal), sticky bar, cart and the CTA labels.

- The hero now shows a visible `h1` "The Limitless Pill." (Boldonse, 34–60px), then "NZT48 Original" in gold, then the rating and the support line. At 320/375 it sits above the fold under the 3D bottle. The sr-only h1 is gone. The 3D anchors are unchanged.
- Presentation fixes:
  - Header uses a tighter layout at ≤400px, so "Shop NZT48" fits at 320/360.
  - The frequency select and `.dd-row` are now 44px tall. Desktop nav links are 44px tall.
  - `.dd-row` inactive alpha went from .34 to .62. It now passes 4.5:1 in both themes.
  - The light-theme hero strike price uses `--c9A9DA3`.
  - Under reduced motion, the announcement bar shows the 4 messages once, wrapped and centered.
  - If the 3D import fails, `html.nzt-no3d` shows every `[data-nzt-fallback]`. This survives re-renders.
  - Fallback images were added to the benefits/formula/facts anchors.

### Bugs found in shared files (not fixed; the coordinator owns them)
- **`variant_label` / `version` is empty on every event after ViewContent.** The PageView call lands on a different `nzt48-shared.js` instance than the one `window.NZT48` ends up pointing at, so the second copy's `VERSION` stays `''`. Observed: `PageView:v1-high-tech`, `ViewContent:v1-high-tech`, then `SelectVariant / AddToCart / InitiateCheckout` with `version:""` and `variant_label:"-dark"`. A direct `window.NZT48.track('Probe')` after load also gives `version:""`. Suggested fix in shared: `var VERSION = window.__nzt48Version || '';` and `if (data.version) VERSION = window.__nzt48Version = data.version;`. V2 uses the same PageView pattern, so it is probably affected too.
- The new `version-switch.js` bottom bar (V1/V2/V3 + Dark/Light) sits over V1's sticky Add-to-Cart bar on phones. Its buttons are 36px tall, and at 320 it is wider than the viewport. It's preview-only, but it hides the sticky CTA during review.
